ECHO LOOP Privacy Policy

Effective: August 18, 2026

This policy describes the data practices of ECHO LOOP. The game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. It does not require a developer-operated account.

Advertising and consent

ECHO LOOP uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent management. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics, approximate location or other data needed to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, or personalize advertising. Review the disclosures shown in the app and Google's privacy information before consenting.

Apple services and purchases

Game Center and In-App Purchases are provided by Apple. Apple may process information under Apple's own privacy policy. ECHO LOOP receives only the information made available through the platform APIs that is needed to provide leaderboard, achievement, purchase, and entitlement functionality.

Local game data

Progress, settings, cosmetics, missions, season state, and gameplay statistics are stored on the device. Optional CloudKit synchronization is disabled in the current release candidate unless explicitly enabled for the production build.

Your choices

You can change advertising privacy choices when the in-app Privacy Options entry is available, disable personalized advertising through applicable system controls, and remove local game data by deleting the app. Purchase history is managed through Apple.

Children

ECHO LOOP is not intentionally designed as a service for children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. Final App Store age rating is determined through Apple's age-rating questionnaire.

Changes

This policy may be updated when game features, partners, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
